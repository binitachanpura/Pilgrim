Where the money comes from

App is 43% of net revenue (₹78,386 of ₹1,83,357) and gives the shallowest discount, 5.5% on average.

Where the discount goes

Amazon discounts deepest at 17.4% on average, up to 22%, and brings in 7.6% of revenue. Its margin is 55%, against 62% on App.

Concentration

Vitamin C Serum alone is 17% of revenue. May 2024 is 23% of the year. With 50 orders, a few large orders move these shares.
